Seller Terms

Earnity · Version 2026-10-25 · Last updated 25 October 2026

These terms apply to anyone who sells on Earnity ("seller", "you"). They are in addition to our Terms of Use and Privacy Policy. By ticking the box when you create your business, you accept these terms.

1. Your business and your store

  • You must give true details about your business and keep them up to date.
  • You get a public store at your own subdomain (for example yourname.earnityug.com). The subdomain can't be changed later and some names are reserved.
  • Earnity reviews every new business. Your store and products are visible to buyers only after we approve it. We may ask for more information, and we may refuse or remove a business.
  • One seller account has one business.

2. What you can sell

  • You may sell only goods and services you are allowed to sell and are able to deliver. No illegal, stolen, counterfeit, unsafe or restricted items.
  • Describe each product truthfully. Photos must be yours or ones you have the right to use.
  • Starting limits for new sellers: a maximum price per product of UGX 1,000,000, a delivery time of up to 14 days, and a limit on active products. We may raise these limits when you build a good record. Limits can change.
  • You are responsible for your own licences, taxes and legal duties.

3. Pricing, commission and the fee cap

  • You set the price of each product and the referee commission. The minimum commission is 1%.
  • Together, the referee commission, any business referrer share, Earnity's percentage and your seller share must fit inside the 48% deposit cap. Earnity's system refuses any combination that doesn't.
  • A business referrer (the person you named when you signed up) can't be changed. You may raise their rate, but not lower it.
  • Earnity's percentage is currently 5% of the product price. We will tell you before a change applies to new orders.
  • Your seller share is what remains of the deposit after the referee commission and Earnity's percentage.
  • You can run time-limited offers (up to 30 days) at a lower price.

4. How you get paid

  • The buyer pays a deposit to Earnity. The rest is paid directly to you when you deliver. Earnity is not part of that payment.
  • Your seller share of the deposit shows as Pending. It is released to your wallet only when you enter the buyer's delivery code.
  • You can withdraw your available balance to a verified phone number. Withdrawals need email confirmation and an SMS code, and large ones need Earnity's approval. Daily limits apply and can change.

5. Delivering an order

  • Deliver within the delivery time you stated on the product. If you have not delivered and entered the code 3 days after that time, the order counts as not delivered.
  • When the buyer receives the item, ask for their delivery code and enter it in your dashboard. After 5 wrong attempts the code locks and Earnity reviews the order.
  • You see the buyer's name, phone and email only after the deposit is paid, only for that order. Use them only to complete the order. Don't share them, and don't use them to market to the buyer or to take the sale off Earnity.
  • Don't ask buyers to pay outside Earnity to avoid fees. Sales completed outside Earnity are not protected by Earnity.

6. If you don't deliver

If you don't deliver, deliver something that is not as described, or cancel an order you accepted:

  • The buyer is refunded their deposit, and the referee's pending commission is cancelled.
  • Your pending share is forfeited to Earnity.
  • Earnity must not lose money on a failed sale. Non-refundable payment processing fees are charged to you.

  • If the penalty makes your balance negative, we take it from future earnings, and your account stays blocked from listing and withdrawing until the debt is cleared.
  • Repeated failures can lead to permanent removal.

7. If the buyer never collects

If a buyer abandons an order after you have dispatched it, the buyer's deposit is forfeited and shared equally between you and Earnity (half each), to cover your costs. Earnity decides each case after reviewing the order.

8. Disputes

Either side can report a problem on an order. Earnity reviews it, may hold the related money, and decides. If a buyer is found to have made a false claim, they may be blocked. If you are found to have made a false claim, or to have given a false delivery confirmation, you may be penalised and blocked. Our decisions on disputes are final on Earnity, but you keep your legal rights.

9. Honest conduct

  • No fake orders, fake accounts, buying through your own referral links to take commission, or any attempt to cheat commissions or discounts. We check for this, and we can cancel earnings, charge penalties and block accounts.
  • No harassment of buyers, referees or Earnity staff.
  • Don't try to damage or overload the site.

10. Your content

You keep ownership of your photos and descriptions. You give Earnity permission to show them on Earnity, in the feed, in your store, and in messages and shares about your products.

11. Suspension and ending

We may hide your products, hold payouts, or block or close your account to stop fraud, follow the law or enforce these terms. You may close your business at any time, after finishing your open orders. Money you owe stays owed after closing.

12. Changes

We may update these terms. The version and date at the top show which one applies, and we record the version you accepted. If a change is important, we will tell you.
