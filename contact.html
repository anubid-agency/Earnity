Common questions

Buying

Open a product, tap Buy now and sign in. You pay a deposit through Pesapal (mobile money or card). You pay the rest directly to the seller when you receive your item.

After your deposit is confirmed you get a one-time code on your order page. Give it to the seller only when you have received your item. The seller enters it to complete the order. You can get a new code up to 3 times.

Confirmation can take a few minutes. Keep the order page open or check again later. Don't pay twice. If it doesn't update, contact support@earnityug.com with your order number.

Referees

Choose products to refer and get your link. When someone buys through your link and the order is delivered, you earn the commission. You can also grant buyers a discount out of your own commission.

Commission becomes available after delivery. Go to your earnings page, tap Withdraw, confirm by email and SMS code, and the money is sent to your verified phone number.
